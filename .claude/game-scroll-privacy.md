# Privacy Policy for GameScroll

**Last updated:** October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GameScroll ("the App") handles information when you use our mobile application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

**We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.**

The App stores the following data **locally on your device only**:

- Game progress and high scores
- In-app settings and preferences (e.g., sound on/off)

This data is stored using your device's local storage (`shared_preferences`) and never leaves your device.

---

## 2. Internet Access

GameScroll does **not** require an internet connection to function. The App does not communicate with any external servers, APIs, or third-party services.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

GameScroll does **not** integrate any of the following:

- Advertising networks (e.g., AdMob, Meta Audience Network)
- Analytics platforms (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel)
- Crash reporting tools (e.g., Crashlytics, Sentry)
- Social login or account systems

---

## 4. Device Permissions

GameScroll does not request access to sensitive device features such as:

- Camera or microphone
- Location
- Contacts or calendar
- External storage

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

GameScroll is suitable for all ages. Since we do not collect any personal information, there is no risk to children's privacy under COPPA or similar regulations.

---

## 6. Data Security

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no risk of data breach from our side. All locally stored data remains under your device's own security controls.

---

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
