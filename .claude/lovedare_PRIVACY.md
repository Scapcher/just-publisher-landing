## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
The only information you enter into the App is the **first names of you and your partner** ("Player Names"), which are used to personalize the in-app experience (e.g., "Alex & Jordan's turn").

### 2.2 Information We Do NOT Collect
We do not collect:

- Real names, last names, or legal identities
- Email addresses, phone numbers, or contact information
- Location data (GPS, IP address, or otherwise)
- Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, or similar)
- Usage analytics, telemetry, or event tracking
- Photos, microphone input, or camera data
- Social media profile information
- Any biometric data

### 2.3 Automatically Collected Information
We do not operate any servers or backend systems. The App does not transmit any data over the internet during normal use. Therefore, no information is automatically collected by us.

### 2.4 Subscription and Payment Information
When you purchase a Premium subscription, the transaction is processed entirely by **Apple's App Store payment infrastructure**. We do not receive, process, store, or have access to your payment method, credit card details, or Apple ID credentials. We only receive a confirmation status (active/inactive) to unlock in-app features, which is stored locally on your device.

---

## 3. How We Use Information

The Player Names you enter are used exclusively to:

- Display personalized in-game prompts and turn indicators (e.g., "Alex's turn")
- Provide a more personal gameplay experience

Player Names are **never** used for advertising, analytics, identification, or any purpose beyond the immediate in-app display described above.

---

## 4. Data Storage

### 4.1 Local Storage Only
All data — including Player Names and your Premium status — is stored exclusively using your device's local storage (`SharedPreferences` on iOS). This data **never leaves your device** and is **never transmitted to any server**.

### 4.2 No Account Required
The App does not require you to create an account, register, or log in. There is no user account associated with your use of the App.

### 4.3 Data Deletion
You can delete all locally stored data at any time within the App:

1. Open **Settings** in the App
2. Scroll to the **Data** section
3. Tap **Reset All Data**

Uninstalling the App from your device will also delete all locally stored data.

If you are a California resident, you have certain rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

- **Right to Know:** You have the right to request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you. As described in this policy, we do not collect or store personal information on our servers.
- **Right to Delete:** You have the right to request deletion of your personal information. Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time per Section 4.3.
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights.
- **Right to Opt-Out of Sale:** We do not sell personal information. There is nothing to opt out of.

To exercise your rights or for questions specific to California privacy law, contact us at [melihbahriaktan@gmail.com](mailto:melihbahriaktan@gmail.com).
